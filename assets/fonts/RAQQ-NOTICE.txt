Raqq — Early manuscript Kufic typeface

Font: fonts/Raqq.ttf from https://github.com/aliftype/raqq
Design purpose: revival of early Qur'anic manuscript Kufic associated with third-century AH manuscripts.
License: GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later (AGPL-3.0-or-later).
The full license text is included in RAQQ-AGPL-3.0.txt.
The font binaries are stored as base64 text assets (Raqq.ttf.base64 and RaqqSura.ttf.base64) and decoded locally when the Quran reader loads. The app can fall back to Amiri if the optional font payload cannot be loaded.
The font changes presentation only; canonical Quran source text and verse references remain unchanged.
